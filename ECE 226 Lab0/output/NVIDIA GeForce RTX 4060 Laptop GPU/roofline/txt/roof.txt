Explaination:
peak FLOP/s = CUs x clock x FLOP per CU per clock

You cannot look up the right-hand side for an arbitrary laptop, and a datasheet peak is
not a number you measured anyway. So run the formula the other way round.

The FP16 and BF16 ceilings are both about 3.5× the FP32 ceiling, and they match each other. 
A ratio near 2× would mean the same FP32 ALUs were simply operating on narrower values. 
A ratio of 3.5× means this GPU has dedicated tensor cores that retire more 16-bit FMAs per clock than the FP32 pipes. 
FP16 and BF16 reaching the same ceiling means they share that 16-bit matrix datapath.
